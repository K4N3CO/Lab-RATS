### Operational Security *(OpSec)* is Crucial.
**If** you find **a hole in the Lab-RATS engine** or **a way to compromise the C2 framework**, **we ask that you to handle it like a professional**.

---  

### Responsible Disclosure:
Do **NOT** open **public issues** for **security vulnerabilities**. **Leaking a bypass** or a **protocol flaw** before it's patched compromises **the entire lab**.

#### Report vulnerabilities via the following channels:
- **Encrypted Comms**: K4N3CO@proton.me
- **Target Response**: We aim to **acknowledge reports within 48 hours** and have a **patch in the pipeline shortly after**.

---

### Scope:
**We are primarily interested in**:
- **Protocol Breaches**: Ways to **hijack the C2 handshake** or **intercept telemetry**.
- **De-cloaking**: Methods that **allow a target OS to permanently flag** or **unmask the app** despite stealth mode.
- **Sandbox Escapes**: Flaws in the **Ghost Controller** or **Accessibility logic** that lead to **system crashes**.

---

### The Reward:
**Your name** in the `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and **the respect of K4N3CO.LABS**. We value those who **build shields** as much as those who **build spears**.

---

*Keep the signal high. Maintain OpSec.*
